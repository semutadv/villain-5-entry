A 96-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very
tired, written for the make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word
Villain Monologue". He has raised legions, unmade kings before breakfast
and plotted this night for three thousand years — and the flawless plan
can start at dawn, because dawn is also fine for doom. The tiredness
never gets announced; it leaks out through the rhythm, the deflections,
a throne that is mostly spikes, and a Dark Lord who would like a pillow
before the trembling resumes.

## Running it

`node main.ts` clears the output directory's contents and writes the
monologue to `/output/entry.txt` as plain UTF-8 text.
